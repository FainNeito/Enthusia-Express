# Museum walkthrough

Tap a role to explore a simulated submission. In game, the server supplies the map pixels, fee and review state.

[Submit art](#submit-art) · [Review art](#review-art)

## Submit art

Open Express → **Submit museum artwork**. For a two-map panorama, enter the title, artist, width **2** and height **1**. Add a description if you wish.

[Next: add maps](#add-maps)

## Add maps

Place the filled maps in the shipping interface. View the submission fee and confirm when ready.

[Submit](#my-submissions)

## My submissions

**Harbor Lights · Awaiting review**  
Waiting for a builder to review your art. Return here to track progress.

[See requested changes](#requested-changes) · [See final status](#displayed)

## Requested changes

Read the feedback and choose **Correct submission details**. Edit the details and resubmit. The museum already holds your maps.

[Resubmit details](#my-submissions)

## Review art

Open **Curator queue → Harbor Lights**. Read the artist credit and review state.

[Preview artwork](#preview) · [Request changes](#requested-changes) · [Accept artwork](#accepted)

## Preview

Look down at one map at a time. For multi-map art, use Previous and Next. Choose Artwork details to return to your decision.

[Artwork details](#review-art)

## Accepted

Use **Collect physical maps**. After collection is confirmed, place the maps in the museum.

[Collect physical maps](#collected)

## Collected

Arrange the maps in their frames. Once they are in place, update the record.

[Mark displayed](#displayed)
